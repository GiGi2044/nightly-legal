Privacy Policy

Nightly · Last updated 12 August 2026

Nightly is a reading app that runs entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What Nightly stores

Your reading history, streaks, reading positions, and settings (appearance, accent, reminder time, interests) are saved only in Nightly's private storage area on your iPhone. They never leave your device.

The library

Nightly ships with a bundled library of public-domain stories, poems, and essays. This content is packaged with the app itself — nothing is downloaded or streamed, and reading it does not send any information anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional nightly reminder, Nightly schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. This uses iOS's local notification system only — no push notification service or server is involved, and no data about your reading is sent anywhere to make it work.

Data we collect

None. Nightly does not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, or location. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete from us — deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Nightly is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Nightly changes how data is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that version ships.
